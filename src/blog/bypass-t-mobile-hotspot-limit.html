How to Bypass the T-Mobile Hotspot Limit (2026)

Published November 6, 2026

Short answer: T-Mobile gives a separate high-speed hotspot allowance on some plans (250 GB on Experience Beyond, 60 GB on Experience More, none listed on Essentials) and slows hotspot to 600 Kbps after it. To work around that, share your phone's data through a local SOCKS5 proxy with Tether Link Utility: your laptop or tablet joins a Wi-Fi network on your phone and browses through it. No root, any device, free to start. Usage still counts against your T-Mobile plan.

What the T-Mobile hotspot limit is

T-Mobile's unlimited plans give you unlimited data on the phone itself, but hotspot data, meaning data used by a laptop, tablet or another phone, has its own high-speed allowance. After that allowance, hotspot keeps working at up to 600 Kbps. That is fine for messaging and email and slow for video calls and downloads. The full table, plus how to check usage in T-Life, is in our T-Mobile hotspot limit guide.

How to get around it with a proxy

Tether Link Utility runs a local SOCKS5 proxy on your phone and creates a Wi-Fi network for your other devices. Traffic from your laptop goes through the proxy and out over your phone's own mobile data connection, so it is not sent through the phone's built-in hotspot feature. It needs no root, works with any device that can use a SOCKS5 proxy and is free to start.

What this does and does not do. It does not create extra data. Everything the other device uses still comes out of your mobile plan, and your carrier's tethering terms still apply, so read them for your plan. What changes is how the data is shared: through a local proxy on your phone instead of the phone's built-in hotspot feature.

Set it up in 3 steps

  1. Install and start sharing Install Tether Link Utility on the phone that has the mobile data, open it and tap START SHARING.
  2. Join the phone's Wi-Fi On the laptop, tablet or other phone, install what it needs first (the guide below lists it), then join the Wi-Fi network shown in the app, with the password shown. It may say 'No internet'. That is normal.
  3. Enter the SOCKS host and port Type the two values the app shows. The step-by-step guide for Windows, Mac and iPhone is here: How to Set Up a SOCKS5 Proxy on Windows, macOS & iOS.

Other ways to get more hotspot on T-Mobile

  1. Move to a plan with a larger hotspot allowance if you tether every day.
  2. Check T-Life for hotspot add-ons available on your line.
  3. Wait for the billing cycle to reset and use Wi-Fi for large downloads until then.
