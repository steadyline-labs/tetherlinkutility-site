How to Bypass AT&T Hotspot Throttling (2026)

Published November 6, 2026

Short answer: AT&T gives each line a set amount of high-speed hotspot data on unlimited plans, then slows hotspot to a maximum of 128 Kbps unless you add more. To work around the throttling, share your phone's data through a local SOCKS5 proxy with Tether Link Utility instead of the built-in hotspot. No root, any device, free to start. Usage still counts against your AT&T plan.

What AT&T hotspot throttling is

AT&T's unlimited plans include a monthly amount of high-speed hotspot data per line, and the amount depends on the plan. After that, hotspot speed is cut to a maximum of 128 Kbps, which is too slow for video calls, streaming or large downloads. Our AT&T hotspot limit guide explains how to check your plan and what your official options are.

How to get around it with a proxy

Tether Link Utility runs a local SOCKS5 proxy on your phone and creates a Wi-Fi network for your other devices. A laptop or tablet joins that network and browses through the proxy, which uses your phone's own mobile data connection instead of the phone's built-in hotspot feature. It needs no root, works with any device that can use a SOCKS5 proxy and is free to start.

What this does and does not do. It does not create extra data. Everything the other device uses still comes out of your mobile plan, and your carrier's tethering terms still apply, so read them for your plan. What changes is how the data is shared: through a local proxy on your phone instead of the phone's built-in hotspot feature.

Set it up in 3 steps

  1. Install and start sharing Install Tether Link Utility on the phone that has the mobile data, open it and tap START SHARING.
  2. Join the phone's Wi-Fi On the laptop, tablet or other phone, install what it needs first (the guide below lists it), then join the Wi-Fi network shown in the app, with the password shown. It may say 'No internet'. That is normal.
  3. Enter the SOCKS host and port Type the two values the app shows. The step-by-step guide for Windows, Mac and iPhone is here: How to Set Up a SOCKS5 Proxy on Windows, macOS & iOS.

Official options on AT&T

  1. Add hotspot data in the myAT&T app if it is offered for your line.
  2. Move to a plan with a larger hotspot allowance if you tether often.
  3. Wait for the billing cycle reset and use Wi-Fi for large downloads.
