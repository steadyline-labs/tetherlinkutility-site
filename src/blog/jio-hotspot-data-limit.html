Jio Hotspot Limit (2026): 5G, Daily Data & Sharing Rules

Published September 18, 2026 · Updated September 26, 2026

On many Jio prepaid plans, hotspot (tethering) usage is not a separate bucket. It draws from the same daily high-speed data allowance as browsing on your phone. That is simple to understand, but it also means a laptop connected to your hotspot can use up the day's allowance much faster than the phone alone would, because laptops download updates, sync files and stream at higher quality.

What Jio's hotspot limit is

Many Jio plans give you a daily high-speed allowance rather than one large monthly pool, and hotspot usage is counted against that same daily allowance. Heavy hotspot use on a single day can use up that day's quota, after which speeds are reduced for the rest of the day until the allowance resets. Daily allowances and tethering rules vary by plan and change over time, so the terms of your active plan in the MyJio app are the ones that apply to you.

Jio Unlimited 5G and hotspot

According to Jio's own help pages, Jio's Unlimited 5G offer requires a prepaid plan of ₹349 or more that includes 2GB/day of data, a 5G-capable phone, and Jio True 5G coverage where you are. Outside 5G coverage, the plan's daily high-speed allowance described above applies.

Jio's Jio Plus terms say there is currently no limit on how many devices can connect to your phone's personal hotspot, while reserving the right to add one later. Jio's public pages do not spell out in detail how tethered 5G usage is counted, so it is worth checking your plan terms before relying on heavy 5G tethering. (Checked September 2026. Jio's terms can change, confirm in the MyJio app for your plan.)

How to check your remaining Jio data

  1. MyJio app Open the app and look at your active plan. It shows the data used and remaining for the current day, and the plan's validity.
  2. Your phone's data counter On Android, go to Settings → Network & internet → Hotspot & tethering or the mobile data usage screen (names differ by manufacturer). Many phones list hotspot usage as its own line, which helps you see how much a laptop is using.
  3. Set a data warning Android lets you set a mobile data warning so you notice before the daily allowance is used up.

For other carriers, see how to check if your hotspot is really unlimited.

Because Jio's allowance is often daily rather than monthly, the practical effect differs from a US-style monthly limit: you may have plenty of hotspot data on most days and only run short on days with unusually heavy laptop use.

Official options when your data runs out

  1. Wait for the daily reset if your allowance resets every day, and plan large downloads across days.
  2. Buy a Jio data add-on (booster) from the MyJio app if you need more data on a given day.
  3. Move to a plan with a higher daily allowance or one that qualifies for Unlimited 5G, if you regularly run short.
  4. Use Wi-Fi or JioFiber/AirFiber at home for large downloads and updates, where available.

Sharing mobile data between your own devices

If you want to share your phone's mobile data with a laptop or another phone, Tether Link Utility is an Android app that does this through a local SOCKS5 proxy. Data used by connected devices still counts toward your mobile plan, so check your carrier's tethering terms.
