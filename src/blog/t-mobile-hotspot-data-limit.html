T-Mobile Hotspot Limit (2026): 250 GB, 60 GB, Then 600 Kbps

Published September 25, 2026 · Updated November 7, 2026

Short answer: T-Mobile's Experience Beyond includes 250 GB of high-speed hotspot data a month and Experience More includes 60 GB, while Essentials lists no high-speed hotspot allowance. After the allowance, hotspot keeps working at 600 Kbps until your next billing cycle. Check what is left in the T-Life app under your line's data usage. Tether Link Utility is built to skip the carrier's tethering limit: it shares your phone's data with a laptop or tablet, with no root and no tether fees.

T-Mobile's unlimited phone plans follow the same pattern as Verizon and AT&T: data used on the phone itself is unlimited, while hotspot data (sharing the connection with a laptop, tablet or another phone) has its own, smaller high-speed allowance. Once that allowance is used, hotspot keeps working at a lower speed until the next billing cycle.

Checked September 2026, based on T-Mobile's April 2025 Experience plan announcement. Treat the table as a snapshot and confirm the numbers for your line in the T-Life app, since allowances change between plan revisions.

600 Kbps is enough for email, messaging and light browsing, but below what a video call, a cloud sync or a large download usually needs. That is why a T-Mobile hotspot can work well for weeks and then feel much slower near the end of a busy month.

UScellular customers are now T-Mobile customers

T-Mobile closed its purchase of UScellular's wireless operations on August 1, 2025. UScellular customers were told they could stay on their existing plans or move to a T-Mobile unlimited plan without switching costs. If you have moved, the T-Mobile allowances above apply to you. If you are still on a legacy UScellular plan, its own hotspot allowance applies instead, see our US Cellular hotspot guide.

How to check your T-Mobile hotspot usage

  1. T-Life app T-Mobile's support pages point to the T-Life app for usage: open the app, pick your line and look at its data usage, where smartphone Mobile HotSpot usage is shown. T-Mobile notes that usage is displayed in Pacific Time and usually updates about every two hours, so a very recent session may not show up yet.
  2. Your phone's own counter Android's hotspot or data usage screen, or Settings → Cellular on iPhone, gives a live estimate.

For other carriers, see how to check if your hotspot is really unlimited.

Official options

  1. Move to a plan with a larger hotspot allowance if you tether every day.
  2. Check the T-Life app for hotspot add-ons available on your plan.
  3. Wait for your billing cycle to reset, and use Wi-Fi for large downloads in the meantime.

Sharing mobile data between your own devices

If you want to share your phone's mobile data with a laptop or another phone, Tether Link Utility is an Android app that does this through a local SOCKS5 proxy. Data used by connected devices still counts toward your mobile plan, so check your carrier's tethering terms.
