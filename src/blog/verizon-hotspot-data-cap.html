Verizon Hotspot Limit (2026): Data Caps by Plan & How to Check

Published September 18, 2026 · Updated October 5, 2026

Short answer: Verizon's unlimited plans have no fixed cap on data used on the phone itself, but Mobile Hotspot has its own high-speed allowance: 30 GB on Unlimited Plus, 200 GB on Unlimited Ultimate, 10 GB on Simplicity and none on Unlimited Welcome. After that, hotspot keeps working at 600 Kbps until your bill cycle resets. My Verizon shows how much you have used. Looking to bypass your carrier's hotspot limit? Verizon's unlimited plans are unlimited for the data you use directly on your phone. Mobile Hotspot, sharing that connection with a laptop, tablet or another phone, has its own allowance, and it is the most common reason Verizon customers find their hotspot slower than expected.

What Verizon's hotspot allowance is

Most current Verizon unlimited plans include some Mobile Hotspot data at no extra charge, but only a set amount runs at full speed. After that, hotspot keeps working for the rest of the billing cycle at a lower speed. Verizon's plan terms describe a speed reduction, not an overage charge. The entry-level plan includes no hotspot allowance.

For most people the number that matters is 600 Kbps: once you pass your allowance outside 5G Ultra Wideband coverage, that is the hotspot speed, which is below what a video call or a large download usually needs.

Verizon's hotspot add-on perks

Verizon also sells hotspot perks on top of eligible plans (Unlimited Welcome, Unlimited Plus and the Simplicity Plan). According to its perk FAQ, the options were 100 GB for $10/month and an "Unlimited Mobile Hotspot" perk with 200 GB for $20/month. The "unlimited" perk still slows down after its 200 GB (up to 4 Mbps on 5G Ultra Wideband, 600 Kbps on 5G / 4G LTE), so it raises the high-speed amount rather than removing the limit. (Checked September 2026. Treat these prices as a snapshot and confirm the current lineup on Verizon's site.)

How to check your Verizon hotspot data

  1. My Verizon app or website Open your plan and usage details. Plans with a hotspot allowance show hotspot usage for the current bill cycle.
  2. Your phone's data counter Android's hotspot or data usage screen, or Settings → Cellular → Personal Hotspot on iPhone, shows how much connected devices used.
  3. Bill cycle date The high-speed hotspot allowance resets at the start of each bill cycle.

For other carriers, see how to check if your hotspot is really unlimited.

Official options

  1. Add a hotspot perk if your plan is eligible and the price works for you.
  2. Move to a plan with a larger hotspot allowance, such as a higher tier, if you tether every day.
  3. Wait for the bill cycle reset and use Wi-Fi for large downloads and updates in the meantime.
