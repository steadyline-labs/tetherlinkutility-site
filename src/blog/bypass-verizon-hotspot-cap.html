How to Bypass the Verizon Hotspot Cap (2026)

Published November 6, 2026

Short answer: Verizon includes a fixed amount of high-speed hotspot data (30 GB on Unlimited Plus, 200 GB on Unlimited Ultimate, 10 GB on Simplicity, none on Unlimited Welcome) and slows hotspot to 600 Kbps after it. To work around the cap, share your phone's data through a local SOCKS5 proxy with Tether Link Utility instead of the built-in hotspot. No root, any device, free to start. Usage still counts against your Verizon plan.

What the Verizon hotspot cap is

On Verizon's unlimited plans, data used on the phone and data shared as a hotspot are counted differently. The hotspot part has a set amount of high-speed data. When it is gone, hotspot does not stop: it slows down until your bill cycle resets. See the Verizon hotspot limit guide for the plan table, the paid hotspot perk and how to check usage.

How to get around it with a proxy

Tether Link Utility runs a local SOCKS5 proxy on your phone and creates a Wi-Fi network for your other devices. A laptop or tablet joins that network and browses through the proxy, which uses your phone's own mobile data connection, not the phone's built-in hotspot feature. It needs no root, works with any device that can use a SOCKS5 proxy and is free to start.

What this does and does not do. It does not create extra data. Everything the other device uses still comes out of your mobile plan, and your carrier's tethering terms still apply, so read them for your plan. What changes is how the data is shared: through a local proxy on your phone instead of the phone's built-in hotspot feature.

Set it up in 3 steps

  1. Install and start sharing Install Tether Link Utility on the phone that has the mobile data, open it and tap START SHARING.
  2. Join the phone's Wi-Fi On the laptop, tablet or other phone, install what it needs first (the guide below lists it), then join the Wi-Fi network shown in the app, with the password shown. It may say 'No internet'. That is normal.
  3. Enter the SOCKS host and port Type the two values the app shows. The step-by-step guide for Windows, Mac and iPhone is here: How to Set Up a SOCKS5 Proxy on Windows, macOS & iOS.

Official options on Verizon

  1. The hotspot perk on eligible plans, a paid add-on with more hotspot data (check Verizon's site for the current price).
  2. A plan with a larger hotspot allowance if you tether daily.
  3. Wait for the bill cycle reset and use Wi-Fi for large downloads.
