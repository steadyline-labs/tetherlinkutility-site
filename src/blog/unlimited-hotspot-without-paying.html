Unlimited Hotspot Without Paying: Data Cap Workarounds That Work (2026)

Published November 6, 2026

Short answer: Nobody gives free extra hotspot data, but you can avoid needing a hotspot add-on. If your plan has generous or unlimited phone data, share it through a local SOCKS5 proxy with Tether Link Utility instead of the built-in hotspot: no root, any device, free to start. Combine it with Wi-Fi for big downloads and you can stay well inside your plan. The data still comes from your mobile plan, so it is not unlimited by magic.

Why a hotspot add-on feels necessary

On many unlimited plans, phone data is generous and hotspot data has a smaller high-speed allowance. Once that is gone, the carrier slows hotspot or offers a paid add-on. If you only need a laptop online now and then, paying every month for more hotspot data can feel like paying twice for the same connection. Our guide Is hotspot free with unlimited data? shows how the big US carriers split it.

Workarounds that cost nothing

1. Share through a local proxy instead of the hotspot feature

Tether Link Utility runs a local SOCKS5 proxy on your phone and creates a Wi-Fi network for your other devices. Your laptop, tablet or second phone browses through the proxy, using your phone's own mobile data connection. It needs no root, works with any device that can use a SOCKS5 proxy and is free to start.

2. Move the heavy stuff to Wi-Fi

System updates, game downloads and cloud backups are the quickest way to burn data. Do them on Wi-Fi at home, at work or in a cafe, and keep the phone's connection for what truly needs it.

3. Lower the data cost of what you do

Dropping video from HD to standard quality cuts the data used roughly in proportion, and downloading shows or maps for offline use moves the cost to when you are on Wi-Fi. Our hotspot data by activity guide helps you estimate what you actually use.

What this does and does not do. It does not create extra data. Everything the other device uses still comes out of your mobile plan, and your carrier's tethering terms still apply, so read them for your plan. What changes is how the data is shared: through a local proxy on your phone instead of the phone's built-in hotspot feature.

Set it up in 3 steps

  1. Install and start sharing Install Tether Link Utility on the phone that has the mobile data, open it and tap START SHARING.
  2. Join the phone's Wi-Fi On the laptop, tablet or other phone, install what it needs first (the guide below lists it), then join the Wi-Fi network shown in the app, with the password shown. It may say 'No internet'. That is normal.
  3. Enter the SOCKS host and port Type the two values the app shows. The step-by-step guide for Windows, Mac and iPhone is here: How to Set Up a SOCKS5 Proxy on Windows, macOS & iOS.

When paying is the right call

If you tether for work every day, a plan with a bigger hotspot allowance is simpler and sits clearly inside your carrier's terms. Use the free methods above for the days it does not.
