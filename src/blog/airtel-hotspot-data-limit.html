Airtel Hotspot Sharing Limit (2026): 5G, 300GB FUP & Check

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated September 26, 2026

Airtel's "Unlimited 5G" plans are easy to misread when it comes to hotspot. Airtel's own terms state that data sharing via mobile hotspot is not allowed on the Unlimited 5G benefit, and that the Unlimited 5G benefit itself is subject to a Fair Usage Policy (FUP) of 300GB per 30-day cycle. Neither point is obvious from the headline plan name.

What Airtel's hotspot limit is

When you turn on your phone's hotspot to connect a laptop or tablet, that device's usage is not covered by the Unlimited 5G benefit. It is counted against your plan's regular data allowance instead (on most prepaid plans, a daily allowance), which can be used up quickly with laptop use. (Checked September 2026 against Airtel's Unlimited 5G terms. Terms can change, confirm in the Airtel Thanks app for your plan.)

The 300GB Fair Usage Policy

Separately from the hotspot rule, Airtel's terms apply a 300GB FUP per 30-day cycle to the Unlimited 5G benefit. After 300GB of Unlimited 5G usage in a cycle, speeds may be reduced for the rest of that cycle. This applies to on-device 5G use and is a different rule from the hotspot restriction above.

How this compares to Jio

On many Jio plans, hotspot use draws from the same daily allowance as on-device use rather than being excluded from an unlimited benefit. See our Jio hotspot guide for the details and its own caveats.

How to check your Airtel data usage

  1. Airtel Thanks app Open the app and select your prepaid or postpaid number to see data used and remaining for the current day or billing cycle, along with your plan's benefits.
  2. Your phone's data counter On Android, the mobile data usage screen (and on many phones, a separate hotspot line) shows how much connected devices used.
  3. Plan details The plan page in the Airtel Thanks app lists whether Unlimited 5G is active on your number and the conditions that apply.

Two different things are easy to mix up: whether hotspot use is covered by the Unlimited 5G benefit (per Airtel's terms, it is not), and the 300GB FUP on the Unlimited 5G benefit itself. If your hotspot feels slow, check your regular daily allowance first.

Official options

  1. Buy a data add-on from the Airtel Thanks app when your regular allowance runs out.
  2. Choose a plan with a larger regular data allowance if you tether often, since hotspot use draws from that allowance.
  3. Use Wi-Fi or Airtel broadband at home for heavy laptop use where available.
  4. Ask Airtel customer care to confirm the current hotspot terms for your specific plan.

Sharing mobile data between your own devices

If you want to share your phone's mobile data with a laptop or another phone, Tether Link Utility is an Android app that does this through a local SOCKS5 proxy. Data used by connected devices still counts toward your mobile plan, so check your carrier's tethering terms.
