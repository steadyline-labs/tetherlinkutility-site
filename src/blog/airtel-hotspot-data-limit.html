Airtel Hotspot Limit (2026): 5G Unlimited, 300GB FUP & Postpaid

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026

Short answers (checked November 2026)

5G unlimited hotspot limit: Airtel said Unlimited 5G is intended for use on the phone itself, so hotspot data for other devices may use your regular allowance. Airtel's published terms were later reported to contain no hotspot clause, so confirm for your plan.

FUP limit: 300GB per 30 days on prepaid, per bill cycle on postpaid. Use beyond it is treated as commercial use and speeds may be throttled.

Postpaid: the same 300GB threshold, measured per bill cycle. No separate postpaid hotspot figure is published.

Airtel 5G unlimited hotspot sharing limit

In July 2026, after users reported that hotspot data was not working with their Unlimited 5G benefit, Airtel said the benefit is "intended only for usage on the smartphone itself" (Trak.in, July 21, 2026). In practice, a laptop or tablet connected to your hotspot may be deducted from your regular data allowance (on most prepaid plans, a daily allowance), which can run out quickly.

The picture is not fully settled. A MediaNama report updated on November 13, 2026 says Airtel's published prepaid and postpaid Unlimited 5G terms list no hotspot or tethering restriction, and that Airtel has made no public statement (MediaNama). Terms can change, so confirm in the Airtel Thanks app for your plan.

Airtel FUP limit: 300GB on 5G

Airtel's Unlimited 5G terms, as reported by MediaNama, set a fair usage threshold of 300GB: "commercial usage is considered when the data usage is beyond 300 GB in 30 days" for prepaid, and the same per bill cycle for postpaid. The benefits are described as meant for personal and non-commercial use.

What happens after 300GB

Airtel reserves the right to "implement throttled speeds in specific cases of commercial use". The terms do not state a speed, and Airtel has not said whether it has throttled any user. The FUP applies to the Unlimited 5G benefit, which is a separate question from hotspot sharing.

Airtel postpaid hotspot limit

Postpaid follows the same 300GB threshold, measured per bill cycle instead of 30 days. Postpaid plans differ in their data allowance and Airtel does not publish one hotspot figure for all of them. Open your plan in the Airtel Thanks app to see the data and benefits that apply to your number.

How this compares to Jio

On many Jio plans, hotspot use draws from the same daily allowance as on-device use rather than being excluded from an unlimited benefit. See our Jio hotspot guide for the details and its own caveats.

How to check your Airtel data usage

  1. Airtel Thanks app Open the app and select your prepaid or postpaid number to see data used and remaining for the current day or billing cycle, along with your plan's benefits.
  2. Your phone's data counter On Android, the mobile data usage screen (and on many phones, a separate hotspot line) shows how much connected devices used.
  3. Plan details The plan page in the Airtel Thanks app lists whether Unlimited 5G is active on your number and the conditions that apply.

Two different things are easy to mix up: whether hotspot use is covered by the Unlimited 5G benefit (per Airtel's terms, it is not), and the 300GB FUP on the Unlimited 5G benefit itself. If your hotspot feels slow, check your regular daily allowance first.

Official options

  1. Buy a data add-on from the Airtel Thanks app when your regular allowance runs out.
  2. Choose a plan with a larger regular data allowance if you tether often, since hotspot use draws from that allowance.
  3. Use Wi-Fi or Airtel broadband at home for heavy laptop use where available.
  4. Ask Airtel customer care to confirm the current hotspot terms for your specific plan.

Sharing mobile data between your own devices

If you want to share your phone's mobile data with a laptop or another phone, Tether Link Utility is an Android app that does this through a local SOCKS5 proxy. Data used by connected devices still counts toward your mobile plan, so check your carrier's tethering terms.
