Mobile Hotspot Limits in Malaysia (2026)

Published October 17, 2026

Short answer: most Malaysian mobile plans that sell "unlimited" data are unlimited for the phone itself, while hotspot (sharing your data with a laptop or another phone) has its own, smaller quota. On some cheaper plans hotspot simply shares the main data instead. What surprises people most is what happens at the end of that quota: on several plans hotspot is switched off until you buy an add-on, rather than just getting slower.

Yes

Yes lists a hotspot figure next to every plan. The 5G Advanced plans with uncapped data (Yes AI 30 on postpaid, which Yes currently offers at RM20 a month for the first 6 months, and Prepaid 30) include 12GB of hotspot. The Infinite postpaid plans step up from 20GB on Infinite Basic (RM58) to 50GB, 80GB and 110GB on Standard, Premium and Ultra. Power 35 (150GB) and Prepaid 20 (50GB) have no separate hotspot bucket: hotspot uses the plan's main data.

Yes's prepaid hotspot add-on terms are unusually direct: once the add-on's data is used, you cannot use hotspot or tethering until you buy another hotspot add-on. The terms list options such as 10GB for RM10. Infinite postpaid has its own hotspot add-ons in the MyYes app.

Maxis and Hotlink

Maxis Postpaid 89, 109, 139, 169 and 199 all list unlimited 5G/4G data, and the comparison page does not state a separate hotspot quota. Every plan is subject to the Maxis Fair Usage Policy, which says that if your usage exceeds typical levels, you may receive an SMS or call about it. There is no published number for "typical", so heavy hotspot use on Maxis is mostly a question of how the policy is applied to your line.

On Hotlink prepaid, hotspot depends on the internet pass you buy. Hotlink's own FAQ lists hotspot as not available on some unlimited internet passes, and says speed is capped at 512kbps after a pass's fair usage limit. Check the pass details in the Hotlink app before you rely on it for a laptop.

CelcomDigi

CelcomDigi's Postpaid 5G 2026 product disclosure sheet lists one line for Monthly All Usage Quota and Hotspot: 600GB on Postpaid 5G 80 and unlimited on Postpaid 5G 100, 120, 140, 160 and 190. In other words, hotspot is not split off into a small separate bucket on these plans, but the unlimited quota is subject to CelcomDigi's Fair Usage Policy, and speed can be reduced once you pass it. Older CelcomDigi plans and prepaid passes have their own rules, so check the plan name on your line.

U Mobile

U Mobile's ULTRA Prepaid 28 gives an extra 5GB of high-speed data for hotspot. Its terms say that after the quota is fully used, hotspot usage is disabled. U Mobile lists a 30-day hotspot add-on (RM5) to turn it back on, available in the MyUMobile app or by dialling *118#. On ULTRA Prepaid 38 and 50, hotspot is shared from the plan's base quota instead of a separate bucket.

Unifi Mobile

Unifi Mobile lists hotspot "up to" a fixed amount on each UNI5G postpaid plan: up to 50GB on UNI5G 39, up to 500GB on UNI5G 69 and up to 100GB on UNI5G 99. Note that the RM99 plan has unlimited data but a smaller hotspot cap than the RM69 plan, so if hotspot is what you need, compare the hotspot line rather than the headline data.

How to check your hotspot usage

  1. Your carrier's app MyYes, the Maxis or Hotlink app, MyCelcomDigi, MyUMobile or the Unifi app show your plan's quotas. Look for a separate hotspot line, not just total data.
  2. Your phone's own counter On Android, the data usage screen often lists "Hotspot and tethering" as its own entry (menu names vary by manufacturer). On iPhone, go to Settings → Mobile Service and look for Personal Hotspot.
  3. Your renewal date Note when your plan renews, because that is when a monthly hotspot quota resets.

For a checklist that works with any carrier, see how to check if your hotspot is really unlimited. To estimate how much you actually need, see how much hotspot data do I need?

Your options when hotspot runs out

  1. Buy a hotspot add-on if your carrier sells one for your plan. This is the simplest fix for a one-off busy month.
  2. Pick a plan by its hotspot line, not its headline data, if you tether every month. The table above shows how much the hotspot quota can differ between plans with similar prices.
  3. Wait for the renewal and use Wi-Fi for large downloads and updates until then.
  4. Cut background data on the laptop: mark the connection as metered and pause cloud sync and automatic updates while it is connected to your phone.

Sharing your phone's data another way

The hotspot quota above applies to the phone's built-in hotspot feature. It is not the only way to share an Android phone's connection: there is also USB, Bluetooth, and apps that share the connection as a proxy over Wi-Fi.
