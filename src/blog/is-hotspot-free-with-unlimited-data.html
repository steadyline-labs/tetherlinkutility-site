Is Hotspot Free With Unlimited Data? AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile (2026)

Published October 5, 2026

Short answer: on most unlimited plans, hotspot is included at no extra charge, but it is not unlimited at full speed. "Unlimited" describes the data you use on the phone itself. Hotspot, the data your laptop, tablet or another phone uses through your phone, usually has its own smaller high-speed allowance. After that, hotspot keeps working at a much lower speed until your billing cycle resets. Some entry-level unlimited plans include no high-speed hotspot at all.

Free to use, but with its own allowance

Carriers rarely charge you for switching hotspot on. What they limit is how much hotspot data runs at full speed. Once that allowance is used, hotspot is slowed to a speed that is fine for messaging and email but slow for video calls and downloads. The phone itself keeps its normal speed.

So the useful questions are: does my plan include hotspot, how much high-speed hotspot data do I get, and what speed applies after it?

How to see how much hotspot you have

  1. Open your carrier's app My Verizon, T-Life or myAT&T, then your line's plan or usage details.
  2. Look for a separate hotspot figure Wording like "high-speed hotspot data" or "Mobile Hotspot" with a GB number is your allowance.
  3. Note your billing date The allowance refreshes at the start of each billing cycle, not on the first of the month.
  4. Check your phone's counter Android shows hotspot usage in the hotspot or data usage screen; iPhone shows it under Settings, Cellular, Personal Hotspot.

When hotspot costs more

  • Your plan has no hotspot. Hotspot may not turn on, or connected devices may not get data, until you change plans.
  • You need more than the allowance. Carriers sell hotspot add-ons (Verizon, for example, offered 100 GB for $10/month on eligible plans in our September 2026 check) or larger plans.
  • You use a separate hotspot device. A dedicated mobile hotspot usually needs its own line and data plan.

Sharing your phone's data with another device

Built-in Wi-Fi hotspot, USB, Bluetooth and proxy apps are all ways to share your phone's mobile data with another device. They differ in setup, speed and battery use; we compare them in 5 ways to share your phone's internet with another device. Tether Link Utility is an Android app that shares your phone's mobile data over Wi-Fi through a local SOCKS5 proxy. Whichever method you use, the data comes from your mobile line and counts toward your plan, so check your plan's tethering terms first.
