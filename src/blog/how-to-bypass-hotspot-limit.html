How to Bypass a Hotspot Limit (2026): What Works, No Root

Published November 6, 2026

Short answer: To get around a carrier's hotspot limit, share your phone's data through a local SOCKS5 proxy instead of the built-in hotspot feature. Tether Link Utility does this: it creates a Wi-Fi network on your phone, and your laptop, tablet or another phone browses through it. It needs no root, works with any device and is free to start. The data still comes from your mobile plan, so check your carrier's tethering terms.

Why carriers have a hotspot limit

Most unlimited plans treat data used on the phone and data shared with other devices as two different things. Phone data is unlimited or generous, while hotspot data has its own smaller high-speed allowance. When it runs out, the speed drops until the next billing cycle. The exact numbers are in our carrier guides: T-Mobile, Verizon and AT&T.

How Tether Link Utility removes the hotspot limit

Tether Link Utility runs a local SOCKS5 proxy on your phone and creates a Wi-Fi network for your other devices. A laptop, tablet or second phone joins that network and sends its traffic through the proxy, which uses your phone's own mobile data connection. It works with any device that can use a SOCKS5 proxy, needs no root, and is free to start.

Settings tricks that change network values on the phone depend on the model and carrier, often need root, and can stop working whenever the carrier changes how it counts. This guide sticks to the route that needs none of that.

What this does and does not do. It does not create extra data. Everything the other device uses still comes out of your mobile plan, and your carrier's tethering terms still apply, so read them for your plan. What changes is how the data is shared: through a local proxy on your phone instead of the phone's built-in hotspot feature.

Set it up in 3 steps

  1. Install and start sharing Install Tether Link Utility on the phone that has the mobile data, open it and tap START SHARING.
  2. Join the phone's Wi-Fi On the laptop, tablet or other phone, install what it needs first (the guide below lists it), then join the Wi-Fi network shown in the app, with the password shown. It may say 'No internet'. That is normal.
  3. Enter the SOCKS host and port Type the two values the app shows. The step-by-step guide for Windows, Mac and iPhone is here: How to Set Up a SOCKS5 Proxy on Windows, macOS & iOS.

If it does not connect

Most problems come from joining the phone's Wi-Fi before installing what the other device needs, or from using the operating system's own proxy setting instead of the setup in the guide. Follow the SOCKS5 setup guide for your device, in the order it lists. To see how your carrier counts hotspot data in the first place, read how to check if your hotspot is unlimited.
